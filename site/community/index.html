Community & Growth

Chirp Community

A collaborative, high-signal space dedicated to real-world usage, architectural evolution, and healthy project growth. We prioritize privacy, security, and respectful pair programming.

1. Principles of Participation

Chirp is built around human trust and mutual respect. To ensure discussions remain constructive and focused on project growth:

  • High Signal, Constructive Feedback: We welcome thoughtful ideas, concrete bug reproductions, accessibility suggestions, and performance optimizations.
  • Code of Conduct First: Every participant is expected to uphold the standards set forth in our Code of Conduct. Disrespectful behavior or trolling is moderated immediately.
  • Responsible Security Disclosure: Potential vulnerabilities or security concerns must never be posted in public discussions. Please report them privately to security@fabrintek.com.br under our coordinated disclosure process.

3. Active Curation & Moderation

To preserve the highest quality of discussion and maintain focus on the roadmap:

  • Spam & Scam Prevention: Because remote assistance tools are frequently targeted by bad actors, comments promoting unverified tools, spam, or scams are promptly removed and accounts restricted.
  • Reaction-Based Prioritization: Maintainers regularly review and elevate topics with positive community upvotes (👍, 🚀, ❤️) to the active development roadmap.
  • Zero External Tracking: This community hub runs strictly on static HTML with 0 external tracking scripts or cookies, honoring your privacy.

Have Chirp?

  1. Open Chirp.
  2. Paste the link under Help someone.
  3. Type your name and press Connect.

Don't have it yet?

  1. Download Chirp, then run it. Nothing to install.
  2. Paste the link under Help someone.
  3. Type your name and press Connect.

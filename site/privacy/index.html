Transparency & Compliance

Privacy & Terms of Use

Chirp is built on privacy by design and radical data minimization: no account, no tracking cookies, ephemeral in-memory connection handling, and end-to-end encrypted sessions.

1. Core principles

  • No accounts required: you do not need to register, provide an email, phone number, or payment details.
  • Zero telemetry: the Chirp application contains no analytics trackers, usage measurement SDKs, or background crash-reporting services.
  • No tracking cookies: usechirp.app uses no tracking cookies, third-party trackers, or marketing scripts.

2. Data controller

Chirp and its public services are maintained by Fabrintek Engenharia Digital Ltda. For privacy and security inquiries or disclosures, reach us at security@fabrintek.com.br.

3. How network data is handled

To connect two computers across the internet without manual port configuration:

  • Connection data (IP addresses): signaling and relay infrastructure temporarily process connection IP addresses solely to coordinate the meeting between peers and to protect network availability against automated denial-of-service abuse. This data is handled ephemerally in volatile memory and is never used to profile, track, or identify individuals.
  • Session content (screen, input, and files): direct session traffic between devices is end-to-end encrypted. Fabrintek servers cannot view, intercept, or store your screen video, keystrokes, mouse input, clipboard data, or transferred files.
  • Zero data sales: we never sell, monetize, or share data with advertising networks or commercial brokers.

4. Legal basis (GDPR & LGPD)

Minimal technical processing is grounded strictly in delivering the service explicitly requested by users (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); LGPD Art. 7, V) and legitimate interest in ensuring operational integrity, network availability, and fraud mitigation (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); LGPD Art. 7, IX).

5. Terms of use & fraud prevention

  • Intended purpose: Chirp is strictly intended for mutual, consensual remote help between two individuals who know and trust each other.
  • Anti-scam notice: Chirp and Fabrintek will never telephone, message, or email you asking for a connection code.
  • Prohibited conduct: using Chirp for tech support scams, unauthorized access, deception, or financial fraud is strictly prohibited. The software is provided under the AGPL-3.0-only license.

6. User rights and inquiries

Because Chirp retains no user profiles or standing databases, privacy rights are honored by design through ephemerality. For questions or vulnerability disclosures, contact security@fabrintek.com.br.

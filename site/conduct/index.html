Community & Standards

Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in the Chirp community a harassment-free, welcoming, and inclusive experience for everyone.

1. Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community an open, diverse, and respectful experience regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or level of experience.

2. Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

  • Positive behavior: demonstrating empathy, kindness, respectful collaboration, and graceful acceptance of constructive feedback.
  • Unacceptable behavior: harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal attacks, and publishing others’ private information without explicit permission.

3. Enforcement & reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported confidentially to security@fabrintek.com.br. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

4. Enforcement guidelines

  • 1. Correction: private written warning explaining the impact of unprofessional conduct.
  • 2. Warning: formal warning with consequences for continued unacceptable behavior.
  • 3. Temporary ban: temporary exclusion from any public community interaction.
  • 4. Permanent ban: permanent exclusion for sustained or egregious violations.
